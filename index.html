Debut Title · Forthcoming Release

The Borrowed Map

Navigating Faith, Ambition, and the Foreign Scripts of Modern Africa

By Olayiwola “Layi” Kola-Seriki

An urgent, forensic examination of contemporary African elite achievement. Drawing from three decades within corporate boardrooms, indigenous fintech, and global transit corridors, the author audits the inherited socio-religious and post-colonial templates that govern modern African ambition—and asks what it takes to build a life on one's own coordinates.

The Philosophy of Coordinates

Why Coordinates?

A map can be borrowed, but true coordinates must be calibrated from where you stand. In an era dominated by imported blueprints, Coordinates Press champions books that help modern African thinkers determine their own bearings.

01 / DISCIPLINE

The Active Insider

We do not publish from ivory towers. We champion thinkers and practitioners who write from deep inside the very economic, theological, and institutional machinery they critique.

02 / SOVEREIGNTY

Intellectual Autonomy

We challenge the unexamined assumption that African legitimacy must always be underwritten by foreign institutions. Our catalogue seeks foundational clarity, not external validation.

03 / ENDURANCE

Books That Outlast

We reject transient social discourse and trend-driven publishing. We engineer books with deep structural durability, meant to sit on shelves and shape generations of readers for decades.
